## Judges' Q&A bank (2 minutes = 3–4 questions; answer in ≤ 20 seconds each)

### Tech and architecture (Krishna leads)
1. **"How do you stop the AI inventing references?"** It *can't* output one. Its output schema only takes IDs of records we fetched; anything else is rejected by code and logged. References come from Crossref and OpenAlex, never from the model.
2. **"What if Crossref and OpenAlex disagree?"** Then it's not Verified. It shows Mismatch or Partial, with the field-by-field table so the student sees exactly what differs.
3. **"Why so many agents? Isn't that a gimmick?"** Each has different tools or a different model, and two pairs *must* be separated: the Skeptic can't share memory with the Scribe it's checking, and the Verifier is pure code so it can't be persuaded. Remove any one and a guardrail disappears.
4. **"Which models, and what does it cost?"** Open models on Groq's free tier (gpt-oss, Llama, Qwen), a different model per agent so free quotas stack. Verification uses zero AI calls; a research session uses about six. Cost: zero.
5. **"What if Groq goes down during your demo?"** Automatic fallback to another model, then Rules mode, which still verifies everything, with a visible banner. Want to see it? *(switch it off)*
6. **"Can it read paywalled papers?"** No, and it says so. Quotes are checked against abstracts and open-access full text; anything we can't read is labelled "Reported, not checked". Never upgraded to Verified.
7. **"How accurate is the verifier?"** On our test set of N references: X/X fabricated caught, 0 fakes marked Verified, Y % of real ones verified. The misses are books without DOIs, which we label Partial. Results are on the Trust Center.
8. **"Does it scale?"** Registry lookups are cached and polite-pooled; DOI lookups on OpenAlex are free. The stateless serverless design scales horizontally. The browser holds each student's state.

### Ethics, governance, privacy (Zedan leads)
9. **"Where does student data go?"** Nowhere we store it. The library lives in the browser; AI calls are scrubbed of personal data; Groq doesn't train on API data; certificates carry their data inside the link.
10. **"Who is accountable if a citation is wrong?"** The student is the author and approves everything. Veracite is accountable for its checks: we publish our test results and have a "report a wrong verdict" path. Faculty can re-verify with the QR.
11. **"Doesn't this make students lazier?"** It's the opposite of a chatbot. Three approvals, an autonomy dial that can turn AI off, read-before-you-cite and explain-it-back. MIT's 2025 study showed LLM-only writers disengage, and we designed against exactly that.
12. **"Why not detect AI-written essays?"** Detectors are biased: Stanford found 61 % of non-native English essays falsely flagged. At a school with many international students that's harmful. We verify sources; we don't accuse people.
13. **"How do you handle biased sources?"** The bias lens shows country, year, open-access and publisher spread with plain warnings, and we show quality *facts* instead of opaque scores, so small or Global-South journals aren't quietly down-ranked.
14. **"Which policies do you follow?"** SP Jain's Academic Integrity Policy v2 (clauses h(vi), h(viii), h(ix), g(vi)), APA's 2025 generative-AI guidance, UNESCO's GenAI guidance, the UAE AI Charter, UAE PDPL and EU AI Act Article 50 transparency.
15. **"How is AI use disclosed?"** Three layers: labels on every AI sentence, an AI-use statement generated from the logs, and a Prompt Ledger. Actually, we used it to write our own prompt log for this hackathon.
16. **"What about prompt injection in papers or web pages?"** All source text is wrapped as untrusted data, instruction-like text is flagged and counted, and the Verifier has no prompt to inject at all.

### Users, design, adoption (Avighna leads)
17. **"Who is the user and why would they switch from Zotero?"** They don't have to: we import and export Zotero/Mendeley formats. Veracite adds the AI research crew and the verification Zotero doesn't have.
18. **"Why 3D? Isn't it decoration?"** The 3D *is* the metaphor: a UV lamp revealing counterfeit marks, a galaxy of your sources. It's off in reduced-motion mode, and every 3D view has a list version.
19. **"What's next?"** An MCP server so ChatGPT or Claude can verify before answering; a browser extension; an LMS plug-in for faculty.
20. **"How would SP Jain roll this out?"** Free, no accounts, works in a browser. Faculty accept certificates with submissions; the library team could host it. Pilot with one MAIB cohort.
21. **"What did you build in Phase 2?"** *(prepare on the day)* What the requirement was, the change in one sentence, which guardrail still covers it.

### Curveballs
22. **"What's the weakest part of your system?"** Honest answer: quote checks are limited to abstracts and open full text, so some claims stay "Reported, not checked". We'd rather say that than pretend.
23. **"Did you use AI to build this?"** Yes, Claude Code built most of it under our direction. We kept a full prompt log as the rules require, and every design decision is ours. It's in our docs.
24. **"Show me it failing."** Paste a fake DOI → Not found, with search receipts. Then: "That's the system working."
